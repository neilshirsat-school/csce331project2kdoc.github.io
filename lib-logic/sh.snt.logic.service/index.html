Business Logic/sh.snt.logic.service

Package-level declarations

Pure business services. Each service is stateless unless documented otherwise, and none of them perform I/O.

  • OrderService — immutable cart editing and totals.

  • PaymentService — tender parsing and payment validation.

  • InventoryService — quantity/unit validation and low-stock status.

  • MenuCatalogService / MenuCategories — catalog lookup, category options, and text filtering.

  • MenuRecipeService — recipe availability and costing.

  • CustomizationService — customization option lookup.

  • CartInventory — derived ingredient requirements and shortages.

  • SalesAnalyticsService — sales summaries and per-employee totals.

val totals = OrderService().totals(
    order = cart,
    taxRate = OrderService.DEFAULT_TAX_RATE,
    discountCode = "BOBA10",
)
check(totals.totalCents >= totals.subtotalCents - totals.discountCents)
